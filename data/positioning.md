# Legalyti approved-fact starter file

This is a starter evidence file for the prototype, not a substitute for founder, product, or attorney approval. The automated idea generator must treat each statement as a company-published claim and link to its source.

## Publicly stated consumer entry points

- The Legalyti homepage currently presents a free start with no account needed: https://legalyti.ai/
- Public legal-help pages invite visitors to start in their own words and label the assistant's output as legal information, not legal advice: https://legalyti.ai/legal-help
- The public EB-1A and EB-2 NIW service pages describe a free first conversation and an attorney-led service path: https://legalyti.ai/services/eb-1a and https://legalyti.ai/services/eb-2-niw

## Public resources for the pilot

- EB-2 NIW requirements guide: https://legalyti.ai/guides/eb2-niw-requirements
- AAO case resource: https://legalyti.ai/aao-cases
- Immigration/talent assessment page: https://legalyti.ai/talentvisas

## Guardrails

- Do not call a tool an eligibility decision, approval prediction, legal advice, or guaranteed outcome.
- Do not invent service availability, attorney capacity, prices, case outcomes, or testimonials.
- Legal claims require attorney review and primary legal sources.
- Do not send user conversations, case facts, documents, or personal information to GitHub Actions, logs, or an LLM.
- Verify every public product claim and CTA before using it in published content.
